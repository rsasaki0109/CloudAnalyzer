CloudAnalyzer

A point cloud viewer and analyzer that runs in your browser. Your files stay on your machine.

Open Web Viewer Guide GitHub

Features

  • Opens PLY, PCD, LAS/LAZ and XYZ files, including clouds with tens of millions of points
  • Streams COPC files from a URL, reading only the octree levels that fit
  • Intensity and classification coloring, with per-class filtering
  • Cloud-to-cloud and cloud-to-mesh distance with statistics and a color scale, exportable as PLY or CSV
  • M3C2 change detection with a per-point level of detection
  • Cut/fill volume between surveys, meshes or a reference height
  • ICP registration
  • Clipping box, cross-sections and cropping
  • Subsampling, outlier removal and ground extraction (CSF)
  • Point picking and distance measurement
  • Merge clouds, split by class or back into their sources
  • Normals with hillshade display
  • Cross-section profiles along a polyline, comparing clouds, with CSV export
  • Share links and session files that restore the view (open clouds straight from a URL)
  • Works on phones and tablets (touch to orbit, pinch to zoom, tap to pick)

Try it

  • Two LiDAR scans — cloud-to-cloud distance
  • Stockpile — cut / fill volume
  • Town — ground extraction (CSF)

Other demos

  • LiDAR map inspection — heatmap and trajectories
  • Browser compare — quick heatmap of two clouds
  • SLAM leaderboard — KISS-ICP, KISS-SLAM, small-gicp
  • 3DGS evaluation — PSNR, SSIM, LPIPS
  • Perception report — batch quality gate
  • Detection & tracking — mAP, MOTA
Data: the map demo uses the BSD-2-Clause hdl_localization sample map with synthetic trajectories; the perception and viewer samples derive from the public RELLIS-3D example (CC BY-NC-SA 3.0). Each demo ships an attribution file next to its bundle.
