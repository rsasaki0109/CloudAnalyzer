Room to explore.

Your data stays local.

∷

Go beyond the preview.

Inspect dense clouds with point budgets, full-detail loading, and COPC streaming. Explore with mouse or touch.

↗

Keep your work moving.

Save a session, share a view of URL-loaded data, and export clouds, measurements, trajectories, and maps.

Explore the full toolkit +
  • PLY, PCD, LAS/LAZ, E57, XYZ and URL-based COPC streaming
  • Cloud-to-cloud and cloud-to-mesh distances, statistics, and color scales
  • M3C2 change detection with a per-point level of detection
  • Cut/fill volume between surveys, meshes, or a reference height
  • ICP registration, LiDAR odometry, and SLAM loop closure
  • Clipping boxes, cross-sections, lasso selection, and cropping
  • Subsampling, outlier removal, and CSF ground extraction
  • Point picking, distance measurement, and polyline profiles with CSV export
  • Merge clouds, split by class or source, and display normals with hillshade
  • Lanelet2 map drafting, editing, validation, and export
  • Session files, URL-based share links, and touch controls
  • Export analysis results as PLY, LAS/LAZ, or CSV
