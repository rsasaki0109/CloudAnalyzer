# Sample Data Attribution

`lidar_reference.pcd` and `lidar_candidate.pcd` are copies of
`docs/demo/perception/reference_scene.pcd` and
`docs/demo/perception/candidates/deep_baseline.pcd`.

- Dataset: [RELLIS-3D](https://github.com/unmannedlab/RELLIS-3D), frame `000001`
  of the Ouster LiDAR example bundle
- License: CC BY-NC-SA 3.0 (see the dataset README)
- `lidar_candidate.pcd` is a deterministic demo baseline derived from the same
  frame, not a model output.

Keep the upstream attribution and non-commercial / share-alike terms with these files.

The other samples (`stockpile_*.ply`, `town.ply`, `slope_*.ply`) are synthetic,
generated by `web/scripts/make-samples.mjs`, and free to use (MIT, like the
rest of this repository).

`nclt-2012-04-29.mcap` and `nclt-2012-06-15.mcap` are parts of sessions 2012-04-29 and 2012-06-15 of
the University of Michigan North Campus Long-Term dataset as ROS 2 bags (MCAP): 190 s of each drive,
once round the same block, every fourth Velodyne HDL-32E scan thinned to 0.8 m, and the MS25 IMU's
roll and pitch at 10 Hz, made by `scripts/make_nclt_bag.py` (keyframes 2520–2740 and 2476–2700 of
`scripts/prepare_nclt.py`'s output).

- Dataset: [NCLT](http://robots.engin.umich.edu/nclt/), N. Carlevaris-Bianco, A. K. Ushani and
  R. M. Eustice, "University of Michigan North Campus long-term vision and lidar dataset", IJRR 2016
- License: [Open Database License](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/), contents under the
  [Database Contents License](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/dbcl/1-0/); this derived database
  is offered under the same terms.
