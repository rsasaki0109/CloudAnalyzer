                IMU gravity

                An IMU's roll and pitch give each keyframe's up direction. Tied to it, the path loses its tilt drift, and the height drift that follows, while hills stay hills. Pick a KITTI oxts folder (with calib_imu_to_velo.txt next to its files) or a text file of frame ux uy uz lines (up in scan coordinates).

                Floor constraint

                Keyframes are tied to one floor plane, which removes the tilt and height drift of odometry on flat sites (a car park, a floor of a building). On hills it flattens the path: leave it off there.

                Join another graph

                A second session of the same place (a folder, or a ROS bag): a node of it that stands near a node of this graph places the whole of it. Their scans are registered with ICP from several headings.

                        For fitted roads, keep the search margin within 45% of lane width. Reduce it when narrowing lanes.

                          Source coverage has not been checked.

                          Orange: insufficient returns. Purple: height disagreement. Marks show failed samples; lines join consecutive failures of the same kind. Missing points can mean occlusion or sparse coverage.

                          Inspect the source before editing. Dragging keeps height; use Edit boundary vertex height to change Z. Shared boundaries update every lane that uses them. After an edit, run Check source coverage again.

                          Draft junction connections

                          Preview connections between open road ends in the cloud's coordinate frame. Ground under a centreline does not establish permitted turns or obstacle clearance. Review branches and traffic rules before adding a draft.

                          Measure a signal from points

                          Identify a signal head, excluding its pole and background. Segment it into a cloud, or enter a tight box in the original metre coordinates. This measures shape; confirm the object and controlled lanes. Lamps and stop lines need separate editing.

                          Import notes
                            Measure crosswalk paint from points

                            Select a road-paint box in original metre coordinates. Retained RGB or intensity is required. Preview measured bands, identify the crossing and confirm its lane IDs before adding. Missing paint can shorten the observed footprint.

                            Lower the threshold to include dim paint. More background can pass; review the measured bands over full-density points.

                            Review equipment associations

                            Select a candidate to highlight the signal, target and road context. Dashed links are preview only. Rejected candidates can be inspected; no target is adopted automatically.

                            Choose targets explicitly after reviewing the map and source. Empty targets stay unresolved. Geometry and lamps are retained; control and phases are drafts.

                            Edit crossing, stop line or signal geometry

                            Coordinates use the original map frame. Drag keeps Z; numeric edits can change it. Observed paint, stored lamps and lane assignments stay at their original values. Review them after editing. Undo restores the complete previous map.
