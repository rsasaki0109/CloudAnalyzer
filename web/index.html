                IMU gravity

                An IMU's roll and pitch give each keyframe's up direction. Tied to it, the path loses its tilt drift, and the height drift that follows, while hills stay hills. Pick a KITTI oxts folder (with calib_imu_to_velo.txt next to its files) or a text file of frame ux uy uz lines (up in scan coordinates).

                Floor constraint

                Keyframes are tied to one floor plane, which removes the tilt and height drift of odometry on flat sites (a car park, a floor of a building). On hills it flattens the path: leave it off there.

                Join another graph

                A second session of the same place (a folder, or a ROS bag): a node of it that stands near a node of this graph places the whole of it. Their scans are registered with ICP from several headings.
